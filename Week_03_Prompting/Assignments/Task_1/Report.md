I chose CAR, RACE and RISE because their structures are easy to understand. I wanted to compare a simple prompt, a prompt with a role, and a prompt that separates the input from the scenario.

I expected CAR to keep the instructions clear, RACE to focus the model on SQL through its role, and RISE to separate the database information from what the model should do.

The full prompts are in CAR.txt, RACE.txt and RISE.txt
